Alignment settings

    
Auto-align looks for speech in the audio and fits your sentences to it. If sentences run together or split in odd places, adjust these and run it again.

    

      
      
      Raise it for quiet or soft voices. Lower it if background noise is counted as speech.

Shortcuts and tips

    

      SpacePlay or pause
      EnterPlay the selected sentence
      LeftBack 1 second (hold Shift for 0.1 s). Right goes forward.
      Up / DownPrevious or next sentence (also [ ])
      HomeBack to the start
      LLoop the sentence while it plays
      SSplit the sentence at the playhead
      TLive sync: press Space as each sentence or word ends
      I / OSet the selected sentence's start or end to the playhead
      PPause or resume (also while syncing)
      + / -Zoom in or out. 0 fits the whole track.
      Ctrl+ZUndo. Redo is Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y.
      ?Open this panel
    

    
Timeline: click the ruler or waveform to move the playhead. Drag a block's edges to retime a sentence, or drag its body to move it. Drag the gold lines to retime words. Hold Alt to turn off snapping. Hold Ctrl and scroll to zoom, or scroll to pan.

    
Live sync: choose Sentences or Words, press Start, then hit Space the moment each one ends. Backspace undoes the last mark and rewinds. With "Trim silence after" on, each sentence is cut back to where the speech really starts and stops.

    
Source text: every line is a sentence. Press Enter to split one and Backspace at the start of a line to join two. The timing of the words moves with them.
